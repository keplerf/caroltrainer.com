Carol explored revenue diversification options on 2026-03-15. The following ideas were researched and prioritized:

1. **Online/Hybrid Coaching** — Add remote clients via Trainerize or Hevy. $100–$300/month per client; 10 clients = $12K–$36K/yr added. Bilingual (English/Portuguese) opens international market.
2. **Semi-Private Group Training** — 2–4 clients per session at tiered pricing (e.g. $60/person × 4 = $240/hr). Niche focus (Women's Strength Over 40, postpartum, etc.). Package as 6–8 week challenges.
3. **Corporate Wellness (Vancouver)** — Downtown location is a selling point. Target mid-size Vancouver tech/professional firms. $150–$250/hr, recurring contracts. Offer: lunch-hour sessions, workshops, virtual team challenges.
4. **Signature Digital Product** — One focused PDF program or nutrition guide sold via website/Gumroad. Passive income once created; $500–$3K+/month at scale.
5. **Niche Specialization** — Specialists charge $120–$150+/hr vs. $61 average. Viable niches: perimenopause, post-injury recovery, executive fitness, postpartum strength.
6. **Affiliate Partnerships** — Recommend gear/apps/supplements already used. 5–20% commissions; supplemental income once audience grows.

**Recommended first move**: Launch a semi-private group (2–4 clients, niche focus) — fastest path to more revenue with existing clients and no new marketing needed.

**Why:** Carol is looking to grow beyond hourly 1-on-1 revenue ceiling.
**How to apply:** Reference these when helping with marketing plans, website copy, or any campaign that could promote one of these revenue streams.
