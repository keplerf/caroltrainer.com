Carol has a DJI Osmo Mobile gimbal and wants to use it for social media content.

**Setup context:**
- Films **indoors** (gym/training space)
- Has **consenting clients** willing to be filmed
- Edits on **CapCut**
- Primary platforms: **Instagram Reels + TikTok**

## Content Ideas & Basic Scripts

### 1. "Form Check Friday" — Slow-Mo (240fps)
Pillar: Education
- Shot 1: Normal speed full movement demo
- Shot 2–3: Slow-mo front + side angles
- CapCut: 3 text overlay bullet points on form cues
- End card: "Save this for your next leg day"
- Hook: *"Most people squat like this. Here's what's actually happening."*

### 2. "30 Minutes in 60 Seconds" — Hyperlapse
Pillar: Behind the scenes + transformation
- Hyperlapse: setup → client working → cooldown + high five
- End card: "This is what a session looks like. Link in bio."
- Hook: *"A whole session in 60 seconds. Still think you don't have time?"*

### 3. "City Moves, You Don't" — Hyperlapse background + static hold
Pillar: Motivation
- Carol holds a static position (plank), gym activity hyperlapsed behind her
- Text: "While everything moves around you… stay locked in."
- Hook: *"The world doesn't stop. Neither do you."*

### 4. "Two Speeds, One Rep" — Slow-Mo + CapCut Split Screen
Pillar: Education
- Same rep filmed twice: normal + slow-mo
- CapCut split screen: left = normal, right = slow-mo
- Annotation arrows pointing to form cues
- Hook: *"One rep. Two ways to see it."*

### 5. "Behind the Content" — ActiveTrack BTS
Pillar: Behind the scenes + relatable
- Show gimbal setup → ActiveTrack following Carol → failed take → good take
- Hook: *"Nobody shows you the 45 minutes before the reel."*

### 6. "Client Milestone Orbit" — Orbit/Spin Shot
Pillar: Transformation + social proof
- Casual client moment → orbit shot around client at milestone moment
- Text overlay: "[Name] hit [milestone] today."
- End card: "This could be you. DM to start."
- Hook: *"She said she'd never lift that. She just did."*

**Why:** Carol expressed interest in using her DJI Osmo Mobile gimbal to create standout social content. These ideas are tailored to her indoor setup, CapCut workflow, and Instagram/TikTok audience.
**How to apply:** When planning content calendars or shooting schedules, reference these as ready-to-shoot concepts with scripts already defined.
